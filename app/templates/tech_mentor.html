Common Field Diagnostic Presets:
DIAGNOSTIC PROTOCOL

Carrier / Bryant Inducing Draft Gas Furnace (Code 31 - High Limit / Pressure Switch Open)

🔍 Ranked Root Causes

  • • Restricted or blocked condensate P-trap / drain line causing water backup into inducer housing
  • • Obstruction or bird nest in PVC intake/exhaust vent termination
  • • Inducer draft motor failing or weak run capacitor (< 3.0 µF)

📟 Target Multimeter Test Readings

Draft Manometer Target: >= -0.65 in. W.C.
Across Closed Switch: 0.0 VAC
Across Open Switch: 24.0 VAC (Tripped)
Inducer Running Amps: 0.85A - 1.45A FLA

📋 Step-by-Step Isolation Sequence

Step 1: Disconnect power and verify condensate collector box and drain trap are completely drained and unblocked.
Step 2: Inspect 2-inch or 3-inch PVC flue terminations outside the building for snow, ice, or debris blockages.
Step 3: Connect digital dual-port manometer in series with pressure switch; turn on furnace and read draft (must exceed -0.65 in. W.C.).
Step 4: Measure voltage across pressure switch terminals: 24VAC indicates switch is open (fault condition); 0VAC indicates closed (normal).

📦 Common Truck Inventory Replacement Parts

Carrier Dual Pressure Switch (HK06WC024) Fasco Draft Inducer Motor (326625-761) Silicone Draft Tubing (1/8-in ID)
⚠️ Field Safety Warning

• DANGER: 120V shock hazard. Disconnect service switch before servicing wiring harness.

• Inspect heat exchanger for flame rollout or cracks if pressure switch trips intermittently during burner operation.
